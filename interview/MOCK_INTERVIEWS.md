# Mock interview rounds

| Round | Time | Hands-on | Follow-ups |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Junior Linux | 40 min | B01, B03, path/permissions inspection | stdin/stderr, quoting, exit codes |
| Backend Bash | 60 min | I01, I02, I04, incident triage | traps, retry safety, cron, monitoring |
| Senior operations | 75 min | A01, A02, A03, release rollback scenario | systemd, containers, SLOs, RPO/RTO |

**Scoring (100):** correctness 35 · edge cases 20 · safe operations 20 · troubleshooting reasoning 15 · clarity 10. Aim for 80+ with no destructive shortcuts.

The interviewer should ask: What is the blast radius? What evidence would falsify your hypothesis? How would you roll back? How do you know recovery worked?
