# Four-week Linux / Bash study plan

| Week | Lessons | Practical outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basics 00–11 | Safe filesystem and process use; complete B01–B04 and project 1 |
| 2 | Intermediate 00–06 | Build tested Bash scripts; complete I01–I03 |
| 3 | Intermediate 07–11; Advanced 00–02 | Network/system diagnostics; finish I04 and release project |
| 4 | Advanced 03–11 | Containers, monitoring, backups, incidents; complete A01–A04 and final project |

**Daily routine:** 30 min reading + command exploration; 45 min independent practice; 15 min failure testing and explanation.

**Graduation:** All 12 labs pass on both fixtures; three project READMEs and test evidence prepared; 75 exercise tasks attempted; final assessment score 80/100 or higher with no unsafe operational shortcuts.
